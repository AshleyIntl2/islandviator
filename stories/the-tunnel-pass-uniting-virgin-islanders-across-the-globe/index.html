The Tunnel Pass: Uniting Virgin Islanders Across the Globe

Ashley Schultz · September 15, 2023 · 1 min read
In 2009, what started as a lighthearted and whimsical concept has since evolved into something far more profound—a symbol of identity, unity, and nostalgia for Virgin Islanders worldwide. The Tunnel Pass, a unique creation that initially captured the essence of island life, has grown into a cherished emblem with diverse meanings for its community.

For many, the Tunnel Pass has become a powerful symbol of transcending geographical boundaries. Whether you still call the Virgin Islands home or have ventured to different corners of the world, the Tunnel Pass connects you to a tight-knit community of fellow Virgin Islanders. A shared link binds people with an unbreakable bond to their roots.

Every two years, a new Tunnel Pass emerges, each with a distinct color. These colors mirror the annual registration changes, signifying the evolution and growth of the Pass. However, an unusual occurrence took place in the unforgettable year of 2019-2020—the fictitious tunnel was temporarily closed. This deviation from the norm became a talking point and a testament to the Pass's ability to evoke emotions and discussions.
And now, as we approach the fall of 2023, a fresh chapter in the Tunnel Pass's journey awaits. Exciting news has stirred the community's anticipation—a Tunnel Pass clothing line and an array of associated products are set to launch. This development adds another dimension to this cherished symbol, allowing Virgin Islanders to carry a piece of their identity wherever they go.

The Tunnel Pass's transformation from a fun concept to a powerful emblem showcases people's enduring connection with their homeland. It serves as a reminder that no matter where life takes us, our roots are integral to who we are. The Tunnel Pass has become a beacon that unites Virgin Islanders across the globe, fostering a sense of community, identity, and pride.
